# ManyChat opsætning — kommentar → auto-DM → /guide (ScoutMePlay)

Klik-for-klik guide givet til brugeren (Aug 5, 2026). DM-tekster linker til https://scoutmeplay.com/guide

## Forudsætninger
1. Instagram skiftet til Business-konto (Indstillinger → Konto → Skift til professionel konto → Virksomhed)
2. Instagram forbundet til Facebook-siden (Indstillinger → Delte adgang / Meta Business Suite)
3. ManyChat Pro (~15 USD/md) — gratis plan kan IKKE lave comment-triggers på Instagram

## Opsætning i ManyChat
1. manychat.com → Sign up with Facebook → vælg Facebook-siden
2. Settings → Instagram → Connect → log ind → giv ALLE tilladelser (beskeder + kommentarer)
3. Automation → New Automation → Instagram → "User comments on your Post or Reel"
4. Trigger: Specific Post (vælg carousel-opslaget) eller All Posts; Comment contains: GUIDE (+ evt. "guide", "5")
5. Step 1: Public reply til kommentar (roter 3 varianter — se tekster nedenfor)
6. Step 2: Send DM med knap "Send me the guide" (button-klik = opt-in, kræves af Meta)
7. Step 3 (efter knap-klik): DM med link til https://scoutmeplay.com/guide
8. Aktivér "Set Automation Live"

## DM-tekster (engelsk, warm voice — genbrugelige)
- Public reply varianter: "Just sent it to your DMs 📩" / "Check your DMs — it's on its way 🎁" / "Sent! Check your inbox 👀"
- Opt-in DM: "Hey! Thanks for commenting 🙌 You asked for our free guide — The 5 Things Every Football Parent Gets Wrong. Every mistake comes with a self-check & a fix you can use the same day. Tap below and I'll send it right over 👇" [Knap: "Send me the guide 🎁"]
- Leverings-DM: "Here it is — enjoy! 👇 scoutmeplay.com/guide — Pop your email in there and the full guide lands in your inbox seconds later (that way you never lose it). One small ask: pick ONE mistake and work on it for a month. That's how change sticks ⚽ P.S. If the link doesn't open, just type scoutmeplay.com/guide in your browser."
- (Valgfri follow-up efter 1 dag, Smart Delay): "Hey again 👋 Did you grab the guide? … Which of the 5 stung the most? Reply with the number — I read every message 👀"

## Vigtige noter
- DM SKAL indeholde en knap før linket (Meta kræver bruger-handling for links i automatiserede DM'er)
- Emails opsamles på /guide → ses i Admin → Marketing → Guide funnel → leads
- Funnel-opfølgningsmails sender kun når der er sat rabatkoder på trinnene
